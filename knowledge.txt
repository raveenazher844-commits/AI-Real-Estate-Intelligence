
AI REAL ESTATE KNOWLEDGE BASE

House Price Prediction:
A house price prediction is an estimate based on property features.
Important features include living area, overall quality, year built,
basement size, garage capacity, bathrooms, bedrooms, and lot area.
A prediction is an estimate and is not a guaranteed market price.

Property Quality:
Overall quality describes the general material and finish quality
of a property. Better quality may be associated with a higher price.

Living Area:
Larger living areas may increase a property's value, but location,
condition, layout, and other features also matter.

Bedrooms and Bathrooms:
Bedroom and bathroom counts influence suitability for families.
Their effect on price depends on the local housing market.

Year Built:
Newer houses may have modern features, but older houses can also
have high value because of location, condition, or renovation.

Machine Learning:
Linear Regression models a relationship between input features
and the target price.
Decision Tree Regression learns decision rules from data.
Random Forest Regression combines predictions from multiple trees.

Model Evaluation:
MAE measures average absolute prediction error.
RMSE gives additional weight to large prediction errors.
R2 measures how much variation in the target is explained by a model.

Recommendations:
A property recommendation should consider budget, preferred location,
bedroom requirements, and other user preferences.
Users should verify actual prices, property condition, ownership,
legal documents, and local market conditions before purchasing.

Dataset Limitations:
The House Prices dataset is based on residential property sales in
Ames, Iowa. Its predictions may not represent prices in Pakistan
or other countries. Currency and location must not be assumed to
match a user's local market.
